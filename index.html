HOW TO PLAY

Hands in. Game on.

Meet the falling ball from below with your index fingertip. Every mode has its own personal best. Every bounce counts. The pace increases every 5 points.

Controls & quick help
Play
Raise one fingertip. Hold it in frame for 3–2–1.
Bounce
Meet the falling ball from below.
Pause / Resume
Use the button or press P.
Restart
Start a fresh round; keep your personal best.
Lives & boosts
Life counts and boosts depend on your mode. A miss resets the ball and clears boosts; your score stays. The 60-second challenge allows unlimited attempts, but its clock runs through recovery. Pause freezes the clock. Stop returns to mode selection.
Stop
End the session and release the camera.
Sound
Optional. Select Sound off to enable, Sound on to mute.

Good light helps tracking. Losing your hand during play does not pause the ball.
