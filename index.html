BUILT FOR A LITTLE PLAY

Your hands are the controller.

Air Juggler turns a webcam and a single fingertip into a juggling challenge. Browser-based hand tracking meets a lightweight physics engine — no controller or account required.

Video frames are processed on your device, never recorded or uploaded. The page downloads fonts, tracking libraries, and model files; personal bests stay in this browser.

How to play

  1. Choose your pace. Start with Easy, try Normal or Hard, or race the clock in the 60-second challenge.
  2. Find your frame. Select Start, allow the camera, and show one hand in good light. Hold it in view through 3–2–1.
  3. Keep it up. Meet the falling ball from below with your fingertip ring. Each bounce scores a point; occasional boosts lend a hand.
  4. Take a break. Pause with the button or optional P shortcut. Restart resets the round; Stop releases the camera and returns to mode selection.

Camera & setup help

Permission or no camera?

Allow camera access using the icon beside your browser’s address bar. Connect or enable a webcam, close other apps using it, then retry. Your microphone is never requested.

Tracking taking a moment?

The first start downloads a hand model. Check your connection and allow model downloads through your content blocker. Cancel or Stop is always available while loading.

Hand not detected?

Pause, improve the lighting, and show one unobstructed hand inside the frame. Keep fingertips away from cropped edges. Losing your hand during play does not pause the ball.

Browser & accessibility

Use a modern browser with WebGL and camera support over HTTPS or localhost. Controls work with Tab and Enter; sound and the P shortcut are optional. Reduced-motion preferences remove decorative effects. Playing still requires a visible hand.
